==== General

Both Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs) and straddle carriers are common equipment for horizontal transportation between the wharf and the yard. Compared to
straddle carriers, AGVs have superior performance in terms of automation in path design, flexibility in adapting to harbour layout, and adaptability of load
platforms to various types of cargo. With the advancement and application of intelligent systems, sensor positioning, and fast-charging technologies, AGVs are
increasingly being used in automated terminals.
