== Overview

[[sec_1.1]]
=== Introduction

The “Data Classification and Encoding Guide” has been developed to provide consistent, 
standardized instructions for encoding S-100 compliant {{PROD.name}} ({{PROD.abbrev}}) ({{PROD.id}}) 
data.

The purpose of the Data Classification and Encoding Guide is to facilitate {{PROD.id}} encoding to 
meet IHO standards for the proper display of {{PROD.name}} information in an ECDIS 
and other electronic charting displays.  This document describes how to encode information 
that the modeller considers relevant to a {{PROD.name}} data product.  The content of a dataset is at the 
discretion of the producing authority provided that the conventions described within this 
document are followed.  A “producing authority” is a Hydrographic Office (HO) or other 
organization authorized by a government, to produce definitive nautical information. 
The entire S-100 Universal Hydrographic Data Model, including the {{PROD.id}} {{PROD.abbrev}} Product 
Specification, is available at the following web site, https://iho.int.
